AI for Science

AI co-scientist.

An AI colleague for research that proposes ideas, checks them against the literature, and runs the experiments. We build the co-scientist and test it against strong non-AI methods on problems it has never seen.

View systems Study with us
The aim

Systems that ground themselves in literature and data, generate hypotheses, write analysis code, plan experiments, and communicate findings, with experts in the loop.

The standard

On each task, at a stated budget: does agent-driven research beat the strongest non-AI alternative at matched compute, on data it never saw?

Domains

Cancer biology, materials and crystal design, combinatorial geometry, and mathematics.

Three strands

Ideas, tests, and a domain to prove it on.

IGNITE

Generating and filtering ideas

A platform that produces original, falsifiable, small-lab-feasible research directions, then attacks them. Each idea must resolve against real prior art, and survive adversarial kill-tests, before it counts as novel.

co-scientist harness

Do agents beat search?

Runs coding agents on a contracted computational task under evaluation the agent cannot touch, against non-LLM search at matched compute, on sealed data scored once. Every number in a report can be re-derived from stored artefacts.

Inverse design

Materials, with the claims audited

Generative models for crystal structures and magnetic materials, built on published methods and checked against pre-registered bars and stability checks.

What we have built

Discovery testbeds where the co-scientist is measured.

Cancer biology

Predicting dependencies

A coding agent outperformed the strongest non-LLM method across most held-out tumour types.

Mathematics

Improved constructions

Agents found configurations that improve on published best-known values, each verified exactly.

Materials

Generative crystal design

Generative models for crystals and magnets with uncertainty-aware rewards and stability screening.
