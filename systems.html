Systems

From models to systems.

The working systems behind the three research focuses: civilisational AI and the science of intelligence, an AI co-doctor, and an AI co-scientist.

Architecture

Each system combines foundation models with retrieval, tools, memory, coding, planning, evaluation, domain knowledge, and expert oversight to support real use in complex settings.

AI co-doctor

Diagnosis and referral support, with auditable evaluation. About this focus

Beyond MAI-DxO

Open sequential-diagnosis panel and faithful evaluator

Our own panel of simulated physicians with a faithful simulator that answers “not documented” instead of inventing results, sealed test cases, and an auditor that reads raw run artefacts.

Capabilities: diagnose, order tests, flag own errors Pattern: clinician-in-the-loop copilot Users: clinicians, health-AI researchers

Visit-by-visit evaluation of whether the system would raise cancer, ask for the right next test and refer, and how much earlier than actually happened, adapted to what each level of hospital can do.

Capabilities: flag, recommend next test, refer Pattern: silent retrospective study Users: paediatric oncologists, referral hospitals

AI co-scientist

From ideas to verified results.

Each system is tested against strong non-AI methods. About this focus

IGNITE

Idea generation with a prior-art gate

Produces falsifiable, small-lab-feasible research directions, then attacks them. An idea counts as novel only after real prior art resolves; a failed search is never read as an empty one.

Capabilities: ideate, search prior art, challenge Pattern: ideation with fail-closed gates Users: researchers, PhD students
co-scientist harness

Do agents beat search?

Runs coding agents on a contracted task under host-owned evaluation, against non-LLM search at matched compute, on sealed data scored once. Every reported number can be re-derived from stored artefacts.

Capabilities: run, score, verify, audit Pattern: pre-registered campaign Users: labs testing AI-driven research
Inverse design (SHAFT+, Magnet+)

Generative materials design with audited claims

Re-implementation and extension of hierarchical generative flow networks for crystals, plus audits of a magnet-discovery report, checked against pre-registered bars.

Capabilities: generate, estimate stability, audit Pattern: generative search with uncertainty Users: materials researchers

Capabilities: discover, construct, invent Pattern: discovery OS Users: researchers, labs
