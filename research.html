AI Future

Civilisational AI and the science of intelligence.

What determines whether a society of increasingly intelligent agents becomes productive and resilient, or unstable and concentrated? CIVITAS-AI is a proposed 2027–2029 programme to find out, with falsifiable predictions rather than simulations alone.

Mission

Establish a predictive science of how humans, AI systems and institutions collectively learn, coordinate and adapt, and test which institutional designs keep human agency and accountability intact.

Status

Proposed programme. Targets, staffing and timelines are proposals, not funded commitments.

Core questions
  • When is collective capability more than the sum of its agents?
  • Are there scaling limits or thresholds in agent societies?
  • Which institutions produce robust cooperation, and which allow cheating or collusion?
  • How do power and knowledge concentrate, and what makes a society recover from shocks?
  • Which human–AI designs improve group decisions without eroding human influence?

Laws and dynamics

Matched-resource experiments on collective scaling, diversity, institutional emergence, failure and recovery, with predictions tested on withheld populations.

Human–AI institutions

Controlled studies of delegated authority, deliberation, independent checking and contestability, measuring human influence separately from accuracy.

AI for Science

AI co-scientist: ideas, experiments, and honest verdicts.

We build the co-scientist and the tests that show whether it beats a strong non-AI method on data it has never seen. A null result is a result.

Mission

Systems that ground themselves in literature and data, propose hypotheses, write analysis code and plan experiments with experts in the loop, held to a standard of evidence.

Status

Research prototypes and pre-registered tests. Results are from internal technical work, not yet peer reviewed.

Findings so far
  • A coding agent edged the best non-LLM method on cancer dependency prediction (+0.025 recall@100), but below the pre-set bar: a null.
  • An early run of new geometric records did not replicate in a later controlled run.
  • An adversarial novelty audit cleared none of 26 candidate ideas.

AI for Health

AI co-doctor: diagnosis and care where doctors are scarce.

A clinical colleague that follows the illness, plans with local resources, and helps the clinician improve. First we make the evaluation faithful, because the standard benchmark turned out to inflate results.

Mission

Shorten time to correct treatment and cut missed serious illness in low-resource settings, measured on outcomes that change decisions, not benchmark accuracy alone.

Status

Benchmark and prototype phase. Not used with patients.

Findings so far
  • Our own diagnostic panel reached 78.8% on 325 sealed cases, against 80.3% reported by Microsoft; the pre-registered non-inferiority test was missed by four cases.
  • The benchmark’s simulated patient invents a result in 44% of requests; a faithful simulator lowers accuracy.
  • Next target: earlier recognition of childhood cancer in Vietnam.

Faithful evaluation

Absence-faithful simulation, sealed test cases, and an auditor that reads raw run artefacts.
