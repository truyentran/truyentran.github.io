AI Future

Civilisational AI and the science of intelligence.

What determines whether a society of increasingly intelligent agents becomes productive and resilient, or unstable and concentrated? CIVITAS-AI, planned for 2027–2029, will find out with falsifiable predictions rather than simulations alone.

Mission

Establish a predictive science of how humans, AI systems and institutions collectively learn, coordinate and adapt, and test which institutional designs keep human agency and accountability intact.

Programme

A three-year programme at the Applied Artificial Intelligence Institute, 2027–2029.

Core questions
  • When is collective capability more than the sum of its agents?
  • Are there scaling limits or thresholds in agent societies?
  • Which institutions produce robust cooperation, and which allow cheating or collusion?
  • How do power and knowledge concentrate, and what makes a society recover from shocks?
  • Which human–AI designs improve group decisions without eroding human influence?

Laws and dynamics

Matched-resource experiments on collective scaling, diversity, institutional emergence, failure and recovery, with predictions tested on withheld populations.

Human–AI institutions

Controlled studies of delegated authority, deliberation, independent checking and contestability, measuring human influence separately from accuracy.

AI for Science

AI co-scientist: from ideas to verified results.

We build the co-scientist and test it against strong non-AI methods on data it has never seen.

Mission

Systems that ground themselves in literature and data, propose hypotheses, write analysis code and plan experiments with experts in the loop, held to a standard of evidence.

Domains

Cancer biology, materials, combinatorial geometry, and mathematics.

Highlights
  • A coding agent outperformed the strongest non-LLM method on cancer dependency prediction.
  • Agents found improved mathematical constructions, each verified exactly.
  • Idea generation gated by real prior art and adversarial challenge.

IGNITE

Idea generation with a prior-art gate that fails closed.

AI for Health

AI co-doctor: diagnosis and care where doctors are scarce.

A clinical colleague that follows the illness, plans with local resources, and helps the clinician improve. We pair it with a faithful, auditable way to evaluate it.

Mission

Shorten time to correct treatment and cut missed serious illness in low-resource settings, measured on outcomes that change decisions, not benchmark accuracy alone.

Where we work

Emergency medicine, paediatric cancer in Vietnam, and district-level care.

Highlights
  • An in-house diagnostic panel with accuracy comparable to published results on emergency-medicine cases.
  • A faithful simulator that does not invent results the record lacks.
  • Next target: earlier recognition of childhood cancer in Vietnam.

Faithful evaluation

Absence-faithful simulation, sealed test cases, and an auditor that reads raw run artefacts.
